Common values:
How many steps the mouse reports per inch of movement. It is in your mouse's own settings app or on its spec sheet; if the mouse has a DPI button, use the value it is set to right now. telemouse needs it to turn mouse steps into centimetres on your desk.

telemouse turns mouse movement into degrees of aim using your in-game sensitivity. Tell it which game and what sensitivity.

Easiest way: press Watch for my game, start the game (or switch to it), move the mouse in it for a few seconds, then come back here with Alt+Tab. Nothing is saved while it watches, and it only reads the name of the program in front.

The name of the game's .exe file, as Task Manager shows it under Details.
The mouse sensitivity value from the game's own settings menu.
How far the game turns per mouse step. 0.022 fits Source- and Quake-style shooters and Apex; 0.0066 fits Overwatch and recent Call of Duty; 0.07 fits Valorant. Unsure? Leave 0.022: it only scales the degree numbers, the recording is the same.

Record 30 seconds now to see that everything works: move and click the way you would in a game. It stops by itself and shows you the report.
